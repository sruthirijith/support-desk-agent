## Return policy
Items can be returned within 14 days of delivery if they are unused and in original packaging. To start a return, share your order ID with support. Digital products and gift cards cannot be returned.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the returned item. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the item was damaged or incorrect.

## Shipping times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders are dispatched within 24 hours on working days.

## Delayed orders
If your order is delayed beyond its expected delivery date, contact support with your order ID. We will investigate with the courier and can open a support ticket. Delays caused by weather or holidays may take an extra 2 to 3 days.

## Order cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge while the status is processing. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery.

## Contact and support hours
Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST. Tickets raised outside these hours are answered the next working day.
